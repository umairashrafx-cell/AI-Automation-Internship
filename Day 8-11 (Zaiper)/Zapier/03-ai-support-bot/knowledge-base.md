# CloudFlow — Chatbot Knowledge Base

This is the entire body of knowledge the CloudFlow support bot is allowed to answer from. Paste it into the Zapier Chatbot's **Knowledge** section (or upload it as a knowledge source file).

**CloudFlow is a fictional software company created for this exercise.** Nothing here describes a real product.

## 3. Refund policy

Customers can request a **refund within 14 days** of purchase.

- The 14 days run from the date of the charge.
- Refunds are issued to the original payment method.
- After 14 days, the subscription can be cancelled but the current billing period is not refunded.

## 4. Support hours

**Monday – Friday, 9 AM – 6 PM.**

Outside these hours, requests are logged and answered on the next working day.

## 5. Common troubleshooting solutions

### Can't log in / forgot password
1. Go to the login page and choose **Forgot password**.
2. Enter the email address on the account.
3. Check the inbox — and the spam folder — for the reset link.
4. The reset link expires after 60 minutes; request a new one if it has lapsed.

### Password reset email not arriving
- Confirm the email address matches the one on the account.
- Check spam / promotions folders.
- Allow up to 10 minutes for delivery.
- If it still hasn't arrived, this needs a support request.

### Workflow not running
1. Check the workflow is **published**, not left in draft.
2. Open the run history and look for the last error.
3. Reconnect any integration showing as disconnected.
4. Confirm the account is within its plan limits.

### Integration disconnected
- Open **Settings → Integrations**.
- Find the disconnected service and choose **Reconnect**.
- Reauthorise with the third-party account.

### Slow performance
- Refresh the browser and clear the cache.
- Try a different browser to rule out an extension.
- Check whether a very large workflow is running.

### Billing — charged twice
This is **not** something the bot resolves. It requires a support request routed to finance.

### Upgrading or downgrading a plan
- **Settings → Billing → Change plan.**
- Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated.
- Downgrades take effect at the start of the next billing period.

## 6. Out of scope

The bot has **no** knowledge of, and must not answer, questions about:

- Anything not listed above
- Specific customer account balances, invoices, or charges
- Refunds outside the stated 14-day policy
- Custom or enterprise pricing
- Product roadmap or unreleased features
- Technical implementation details of CloudFlow's own systems
- Comparisons with competitor products

For anything in this list, the bot uses the fallback response and offers to create a support request.
