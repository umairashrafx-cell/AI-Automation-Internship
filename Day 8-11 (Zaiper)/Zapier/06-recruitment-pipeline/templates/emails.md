# Module 06 — Recruitment Email Templates

Generated by [`../code/stage-actions.js`](../code/stage-actions.js) and [`../code/stale-candidate-check.js`](../code/stale-candidate-check.js). This file is the readable copy — edit wording in the code file, since that is what the Zap actually runs.

Company name is set at the top of `stage-actions.js` (`COMPANY`), along with `HR_EMAIL` and `RECRUITER_EMAIL`.

---

## 1. Screening — Applied → Screening

**To:** candidate

```
Subject: Your application is being reviewed — {{position}}

Hi {{candidateName}},

Thanks for applying for {{position}} at {{COMPANY}}.

Your application has passed our initial checks and is now with our team
for screening. We aim to come back to you within 3 working days.

In the meantime, if you have anything else you would like us to see —
a portfolio link, a project, a repository — simply reply to this email.

Reference: {{candidateId}}
{{COMPANY}} Recruitment
```

## 2. Technical interview — Screening → Technical Interview

**To:** candidate

```
Subject: Technical interview — {{position}} at {{COMPANY}}

Hi {{candidateName}},

Good news — we would like to invite you to a technical interview for
{{position}}.

Format:    60 minutes, video call
Covers:    a walkthrough of your experience, then a practical exercise
Prepare:   be ready to talk through something you have built

Please reply with two or three time slots that suit you over the next
week and we will send a calendar invitation.

Reference: {{candidateId}}
{{COMPANY}} Recruitment
```

## 3. HR notification — Technical Interview → HR Interview

**To:** `hr@company.com` — **internal, not the candidate**

```
Subject: Candidate ready for HR interview — {{candidateName}} ({{position}})

A candidate has cleared the technical interview and is ready for HR.

Candidate: {{candidateName}}
Position:  {{position}}
Priority:  {{priority}}
Email:     {{email}}
Ref:       {{candidateId}}

Please schedule the HR interview and update the board.
```

---

## 4. Offer — HR Interview → Offer

**No automated message.**

An offer involves a salary, a start date, and a negotiation. It is made by a person. The board records that the stage was reached; HR follows up directly.

## 6. Rejection — Any stage → Rejected

**To:** candidate

```
Subject: Update on your application — {{position}}

Hi {{candidateName}},

Thank you for taking the time to apply for {{position}} at {{COMPANY}},
and for the effort you put into your application.

After careful consideration we have decided not to move forward on this
occasion. This was a competitive process and the decision was not an easy
one.

We will keep your details on file and would welcome an application from
you for future openings.

We wish you the very best with your search.

Reference: {{candidateId}}
{{COMPANY}} Recruitment
```

**Why this one is checked first in the router:** `Rejected` can be reached from any column, including `Offer`. Evaluating it before the other stages means the email fires wherever the card came from — which is what *"Any stage → Rejected"* requires.

## 7. Stale candidate nudge — extra challenge

**To:** recruiter
**Trigger:** daily sweep, no update for 5+ days, non-terminal stage

```
Subject: No update for {{daysSinceUpdate}} days — {{candidateName}} ({{stage}})

{{candidateName}} has been waiting {{daysSinceUpdate}} days without an update.

Candidate: {{candidateName}}
Position:  {{position}}
Stage:     {{stage}}
Priority:  {{priority}}
Ref:       {{candidateId}}

Candidates should hear something at least every 5 days.
Either move the card or send them a holding note.
```

## Writing notes

- **Rejections get more care than acceptances.** The rejection template is the longest one here on purpose — it names the effort, avoids "unfortunately", and leaves a door open. It is also the message most candidates will ever receive from you.
- **Never promise a timeline the team cannot hold.** "Within 3 working days" appears once, in screening; nowhere else commits to a date.
- **Reference IDs on every message** so a reply can be matched to a record.
- **Internal messages state the fact first** (`A candidate has cleared…`); candidate messages open with the person.
